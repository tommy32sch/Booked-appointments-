Medical and trauma assessment — practice

22 type-in facts. Tap to reveal.

Tap a card to reveal the answer.

1. First two lines of every oral station opening?
Answer
BSI. Is my scene safe?
Why
BSI. Is my scene safe? BSI and scene safety is the first sentence of every station.
2. After BSI/scene, medical asks NOI. Trauma asks what instead?
Answer
mechanism of injury / MOI
Why
What is the nature of illness? (Trauma: what is the mechanism of injury?) MOI instead of NOI.
3. Name the rest of the opening after NOI/MOI.
Answer
how many patients; request EMS / consider ALS; consider C-spine
Why
How many patients? Request additional EMS / consider ALS. Consider C-spine.
4. Eyes closed for LOC — verbal or pain first?
Answer
verbal first, then pain
Why
Eyes closed: verbal first. Then pain. Do not start with a sternal rub.
5. What does X mean in XABC?
Answer
huge bleed / sucking chest / kills in the next minute
Why
X = huge bleed, sucking chest, anything that kills in the next minute. Treat it now.
6. Sucking chest hole — first hand move, then dressing?
Answer
gloved hand, then plastic 3 sides
Why
Sucking hole → gloved hand, then plastic 3 sides.
7. Trauma unconscious airway open — which maneuver?
Answer
jaw thrust
Why
Trauma = jaw thrust.
8. Rate 8 and shallow — what breathing treatment?
Answer
BVM, 15 L, one breath every 6 seconds
Why
Rate 8 and shallow → BVM, 15 L, one breath every 6 seconds.
9. Cool pale clammy + fast weak pulse — what is it, and the package?
Answer
shock; high-flow oxygen, keep warm, load and go
Why
Cool pale clammy + fast weak pulse = shock. High-flow oxygen, keep warm, load and go. Shock package: oxygen, supine, warm, rapid transport.
10. Medical owes four extras trauma does not. Name them.
Answer
SAMPLE history, matching secondary, full vitals, field impression + treatments
Why
History SAMPLE (±OPQRST), secondary exam, full vitals, field impression then treatments.
11. Unstable vs stable reassess times?
Answer
unstable every 5 minutes; stable every 15
Why
Reassess. Unstable every 5 minutes. Stable every 15.
12. On trauma, do you give a field impression?
Answer
no
Why
No field impression. No calling the hospital to tell them my diagnosis.
13. Order of vitals vs secondary on trauma — what matters?
Answer
order can swap; do not forget vitals
Why
Order of vitals vs secondary can swap; do not forget vitals.
14. Expand DCAP-BTLS.
Answer
deformities, contusions, abrasions, punctures, burns, tenderness, lacerations, swelling
Why
Deformities. Contusions. Abrasions. Punctures. Burns. Tenderness. Lacerations. Swelling.
15. Prone, unresponsive, blood in mouth, gurgling — sequence?
Answer
roll, jaw thrust, suction, OPA
Why
Prone, unresponsive, blood in the mouth, gurgling → roll, jaw thrust, suction, OPA.
16. Trachea shifting + silent one side — needle or keep bagging?
Answer
keep bagging; we do not needle
Why
Trachea starting to shift + silent one side → tension in your head. Keep bagging. We do not needle.
17. Unstable pelvis + closed tib-fib — what do you bind, and the leg?
Answer
binder the pelvis; note the leg
Why
Binder the pelvis. Note the leg. Do not stop the truck for a pretty splint.
18. Suction time limit in the airway?
Answer
≤ 10 seconds
Why
Vomit / blood / teeth / gurgling = suction ≤ 10 seconds.
19. No gag vs gag for airway adjunct?
Answer
no gag → OPA; gag / no face trauma → NPA
Why
No gag → OPA. Gag / no face trauma → NPA.
20. Written "what first?" maps to what?
Answer
this sheet’s order
Why
Written question what first? = this sheet’s order, not do everything at once.
21. When you board/move trauma, what do you scan?
Answer
the back while you roll
Why
Board them when you move. Scan the back while you roll.
22. Call medical control for drugs unless what?
Answer
they tell you that you have standing orders
Why
Call medical control for drugs unless they tell you that you have standing orders. On written tests the safe answer is usually call.
