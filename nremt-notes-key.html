Chapter 1 — EMS systems


Chapter 1: EMS Systems — NREMT key (drill)


**Sources:** HIS Ch 1 lecture + study guide only.


**[Scene Size-up and Safety]**

  • Be safe **before** responding / getting to the patient.
  • Scene-size-up steps / named hazards / PPE: **not in his notes / don’t invent.**

    • **[Operations]**

  • **1966** White Paper: high trauma deaths → modern EMS
  • **1970** NHTSA under DOT
  • **1971** AAOS first EMT textbook
  • White Paper title beyond “White Paper”: **not in his notes / don’t invent.**
  • Shift: pure transport → healthcare + medical oversight
  • EMR ~**50** h: CPR + first aid
  • EMT ~**130–150** h: BLS, limited meds
  • AEMT: IVs + expanded meds; hours **not in his notes / don’t invent.**
  • Paramedic ALS **≥1,000** h: advanced airways, EKG, **55–60** meds
  • EMT + paramedic work under **Medical Director’s license**; MD is **legally responsible**
  • AZ historically EMT + Paramedic only; EMR/AEMT recent, limited jobs
  • Urban: advanced skills usually paramedic-only
  • Rural: some trained EMTs start IVs **with medical director approval**
  • Skills-based: know right, do wrong (O2 **2 LPM** not **15**)
  • Rules-based: right rule, **wrong situation** (stroke as low sugar)
  • Knowledge-based: new + incomplete → wrong plan (wrong antidote)
  • **Primary** prevention: stop before it starts (vaccines, seatbelts, education)
  • **Secondary:** detect early (screenings; early HTN/cholesterol Rx)
  • Tertiary: **not in his notes / don’t invent.**

    • **[Patient Treatment and Transport]**

  • Offline = standing orders, no phone: CPR/AED, O2, epi for anaphylaxis; most routine
  • Online = radio/phone; one situation only; new call each time; unsure / unusual / extra auth
  • Advocacy: best interest; speak up; honor DNR/wishes; explain; privacy/dignity; report errors
  • Capacity → may refuse Rx **and** transport even if badly hurt (partially amputated leg)
  • Assess capacity carefully; **document everything**
  • Later unconscious / lose capacity → **implied consent** → treat and transport
  • Capacity test steps / extra consent types: **not in his notes / don’t invent.**



    • Chapter 2 — Safety and wellness


    Chapter 2: Workplace Safety and Wellness — NREMT key (drill)


    **Sources:** HIS Ch 2 lecture + study guide only.


    **[Scene Size-up and Safety]**

  • You first. Cannot help if you become a patient.
  • Front-door survey: look, listen, smell, mood. Re-scan after every task. Trust your gut.
  • Police: violence / weapons / threats / crime / aggressive people / suspected abuse.
  • Hazmat: spills, unknown substances, chemical fumes, toxic exposure, industrial hazmat.
  • Pathogen / contamination / exposure / transmission.
  • Routes: direct, indirect, droplet, airborne (**TB, measles**), vector.
  • Standard precautions every call. Always: hand hygiene + gloves. Add eyes/mask/gown/N95 as needed.
  • Sanitizer **≥60%** if not dirty. Soap/water if soiled or **norovirus / C. diff**.
  • Don: hygiene → gown → mask → eyes → gloves. Doff: gloves → gown → eyes → mask → hygiene.
  • N95 = airborne + **fit-tested**. Surgical mask = droplets. Never recap needles.
  • Match other providers’ PPE level on scene.
  • Safety order: **you** (gloves every time) → **crew** → **patient**.
  • Park **100 ft** fires, crashes, hazmat. First unit **upstream**; later **downstream**.
  • Hazmat: **uphill / upwind**. NREMT: **ERG**.
  • If not safe, do not enter.

    • **[Primary Assessment]**

  • Fight-or-flight: sympathetic + stress hormones.
  • Fast HR/BP, fast breathing, dilated pupils, blood to muscles/heart/lungs/brain, slow digestion, tense, sweat, less pain, heightened senses.
  • Can look like shock (pale/cool/sweaty, nausea, anxiety).
  • Named hormones: **not in his notes / don’t invent.**

    • **[Operations]**

  • Acute (during/right after) vs delayed (days–months: nightmares, flashbacks) vs cumulative (burnout).
  • Goal: prevent PTSD/burnout. CISM steps: **not in his notes / don’t invent.**
  • Infection-control plan: exposure determination, compliance methods, Hep B vax, post-exposure follow-up, training, records/sharps log, cleaning.
  • Exposure: hand off care → wash/flush → **do not** squeeze/suck/bleach → report immediately → eval within hours → PEP if indicated, **no cost to you**.
  • Cannot force new source blood in most cases. Exact PEP drugs/hours: **not in his notes / don’t invent.**

    • **[Patient Treatment and Transport]**

  • Critical patient: introduce, orient, honest not cruel, hope without false hope, notify family.
  • Grief: denial, anger, bargaining, depression, acceptance — not always in order.



    • Chapter 3 — Medical, legal, ethical


    Chapter 3: Medical, Legal, and Ethical Issues — NREMT key (drill)


    **Sources:** HIS Ch 3 lecture + study guide only.


    **[Operations]**

  • Good faith + appropriate standard of care → usually avoids legal exposure.
  • Standard of care = reasonably prudent EMT, similar training, same situation.
  • Negligence = **duty + breach + damages + causation** (all four).
  • Abandonment = leave without consent and without equal/higher handoff while they still need care.
  • Confidentiality: care team / required by law (GSW, abuse, public health, crimes) / court / written permission.
  • Not family (unless allowed), not media, not social.
  • HIPAA by name / off-duty duty-to-act: **not in his notes / don’t invent.**

    • **[Patient Treatment and Transport]**

  • Expressed = alert + capacity + agree (verbal/nonverbal), must be informed.
  • Informed = complete expressed (nature, risks, benefits, alternatives). All informed is expressed; not all expressed is informed.
  • Implied = unresponsive/altered, true emergency, reasonable-person assumption.
  • Involuntary = legal authority (hold, court, danger to self/others).
  • Capacity → refuse even if they may die. No capacity + emergency → implied, treat.
  • Valid refusal: capacity, informed, voluntary, signature when possible.
  • Capacity test: understand, appreciate consequences, reason, communicate a choice.
  • Before leaving: reassess, confirm, encourage 911, responsible person, sign or document refusal to sign, med control if required.
  • Living will often not enough for EMS to withhold. Need valid **DNR/POLST**.
  • Conscious capacity overrides paper. Doubt → treat + med control.
  • AZ DNR form rules / hold statutes: **not in his notes / don’t invent.**

    • **[Scene Size-up and Safety]**

  • Restraint last resort: danger, protect, allow care, less-restrictive failed.
  • Never prone, never hog-tie. Soft = EMS preferred. Chemical = ALS, not normal EMT.
  • Monitor ABCs. Med control if possible. LE when possible.

    • **[Primary Assessment]**

  • Presumptive ≠ withhold (unresponsive, no breathing, no pulse, fixed dilated pupils).
  • Definitive: lividity, rigor, decomposition, incompatible injuries — or valid DNR/POLST.
  • Doubt → **CPR**.
  • ME: unexpected, trauma/violence/suicide, suspicious, no recent physician. LE first. Don’t move the body.



    • Chapter 4 — Communication and PCR


    Chapter 4: Communication and Documentation — NREMT key (drill)


    **Sources:** HIS Ch 4 lecture + study guide only.


    **[Secondary Assessment]**

  • Open-ended first, then closed-ended.
  • Confrontation / interpretation / explanation / summary — know the examples.
  • Avoid: false reassurance, leading questions, advice, interrupting, jargon, judgment, talking over the patient.
  • Children / special needs: their level, involve caregivers, simple language.

    • **[Patient Treatment and Transport]**

  • 10 golden rules: eye contact; names; truth; plain language; careful talk about them; body language; slow/clear; face hearing-impaired; time to answer; calm confidence.
  • Transfer only after verbal handoff to **equal or higher** + completed PCR.

    • **[Operations]**

  • Radio: brief, plain English, **no patient name**, repeat orders back.
  • SBAR: Situation, Background, Assessment, Recommendation/ETA.
  • PCR is legal + permanent. Include demographics, CC, history, vitals, findings, treatments, response, times. Facts only. Not documented = didn’t happen.
  • PCR error: one line, initial, date, correct. Never erase.
  • Refusal on PCR: capacity, risks, understanding, signature or refusal to sign.
  • 10-codes / CHART/SOAP / ePCR steps: **not in his notes / don’t invent.**



    • Chapter 5 — Medical terms


    Chapter 5: Medical Terminology — NREMT key (drill)


    **Sources:** HIS Ch 5 lecture + study guide only.


    **[Secondary Assessment]**

  • Build: prefix + root + (o) + suffix
  • tachy fast, brady slow, hyper high, hypo low, a/an without, dys difficult/painful/abnormal, pre before, post after
  • Roots: cardi heart, hepat liver, nephr kidney, neur nerve, psych mind, thorac chest
  • Suffixes: itis inflammation, pathy disease, algia pain, ectomy removal, megaly enlargement, ology study of, osis abnormal condition, emia blood, pnea breathing, phagia swallow/eat
  • Numbers: uni 1, bi 2, tri 3, quadri 4, multi/poly many, nulli none, hemi/semi half
  • Colors: cyan blue, erythr red, leuk white, melan black, poli gray, cirrh/jaund/xanth yellow
  • ab vs ad; intra vs inter; endo vs ecto/exo; ipsi vs contra; peri vs circum
  • Directions (patient’s L/R): superior/inferior, anterior-ventral / posterior-dorsal, medial/lateral, proximal/distal, superficial/deep
  • Move: flex/extend, abd/add, rotate, circumduct, supinate/pronate, plantar flex / dorsiflex
  • Extra roots/abbreviations not listed: **not in his notes / don’t invent.**

    • **[Patient Treatment and Transport]**

  • Positions: supine, prone, recovery/lateral recumbent, Fowler **45–90**, semi-Fowler **30–45**, Trendelenburg rarely used, shock position older/caution
  • Document found/transported position.

    • Saturday — Circulatory + airway


    Saturday AM: Circulatory + Airway — NREMT key (drill)


    **Sources:** HIS 8:02 intro, 8:33 fundamentals, 9:02 live note, 9:32 airway, combined pack only.


    **[Scene Size-up and Safety]**

  • Scene safety always first. You become a patient → you help nobody.
  • Adult: go to **patient** first. Child: go to **parent** first if present.

    • **[Primary Assessment]**

  • Pump + pipes + fluid. Fail any one → no circulation/BP. Goal: O2 + sugar to every cell.
  • HR 60–100 adult rest (avg 70). >100 tachy, <60 brady. Athletes in 40s can be OK.
  • SV = one beat. CO = SV × HR (one minute). Field: **BP, skin, mentation** — no volume gadget.
  • 80 mL × 70 = **5.6 L/min**. Body ~**5–6 L**. Three 2-L sodas.
  • Aorta first. Rupture → fatal in **seconds** (as he said).
  • Bleed → low preload → low BP → can pass out. Brain hates low **O2, sugar, temperature**.
  • Afterload = pipe size / SVR. Constrict ↑P, dilate ↓P. Anaphylaxis = dilated; **Epi constricts**.
  • Pulse uses: dead/CPR vs rate vs **distal to injury**. Pulse = LV wave.
  • BP: top = LV **pump** (ex. 120); bottom = LV **relax/fill** (ex. 80). Cuff steps: **not in his notes / don’t invent.**
  • Arterial: bright + **spurting**. Venous: darker + **ooze**. IV = vein.
  • ABC first. No airway → no good patient. Fix **A** before you move on.
  • Most common block: **tongue** (unconscious).
  • Trauma head/neck/spine: **jaw thrust, no head tilt**. Medical: **head-tilt chin-lift**.
  • OPA = **no gag**. NPA = **has gag**; measure **nose → earlobe**, lube, twist.
  • Look, listen, feel. SpO2 goal **≥94%**.
  • NC **2–6**; NRB **10–15** (most patients); BVM **15** if inadequate/apneic.
  • Suction **out only**, only while pulling stuff. Adult **15** / child **10** / infant **5** s.
  • Recovery: unconscious **and breathing** → **side**.
  • Distress = still moving air. Arrest = stopped. Treat the same: open, O2, assist, watch SpO2, listen.

    • **[Secondary Assessment]**

  • Pulmonary = to lungs. Systemic = to body. RA deox → RV → lungs → LA ox → LV → body.
  • Cardiac muscle makes its own electricity. Unconscious ≠ heart stops.
  • Atria together, then ventricles. LV thicker. Weak LV → backup to lungs / SOB (CHF preview).
  • Valves TPMA: tri (R, 3) → pulmonic → bi/mitral (L, 2) → aortic. Sounds = slam shut. MVP = inverts.
  • SVC top of RA; IVC bottom. Pulm arteries deox away; pulm veins ox back.
  • SA node = **primary pacemaker** (sinus). AV = pause to fill. EKG read = medic.
  • Arteries away. Deox arteries: **pulmonary** (+ fetal umbilical arteries). Umbilical vein is ox.
  • Coronaries off aorta feed the muscle. Clog = MI. Chamber blood does **not** feed the heart.
  • Hepat- = liver; detox; trauma = big bleed (~40% volume preview). Renal = kidneys filter.
  • Mesentery suspends gut + vessels.
  • RBC ~**120 days**. Bones make. Spleen kills old ones (not “filter” like kidneys).
  • Gas exchange **only at capillaries**. Meet alveoli. Diffusion.
  • Sites: carotid, radial, femoral, brachial vs humerus, top of foot, behind/inside ankle bump. Latin names: **not in his notes / don’t invent.**
  • Solid (spleen, liver, pancreas, kidneys) = **bleed**. Hollow (e.g. stomach) = **infection**.
  • Quadrants: cross at **belly button**. Patient’s L/R. Spleen = **LUQ**. Full organ lists: **not in his notes / don’t invent.**
  • Plasma + formed elements: RBC, WBC, platelets.

    • **[Patient Treatment and Transport]**

  • Blood thinners = no clot. Good for clot risk, bad if bleeding. Daily BP meds can blunt fight-or-flight.
  • EpiPen job he named here: **massive vasoconstriction**.
  • O2 unless a reason not to (reasons: **not in his notes / don’t invent.**).
  • Disease-specific Rx (asthma, COPD, CPAP, nebs): **not in his notes / don’t invent.**

    Sat Aug 22 endocrine / nervous / GI — NREMT key (drill)


    **Sources:** HIS 10:19 + 10:48 + later pack only.


    **[Primary Assessment]**

  • Adrenals = pyramids **on kidneys**. Adrenaline = **epinephrine** (norepi smashed in).
  • **Alpha** = vasoconstrict. **β1** = 1 heart, ↑HR + contractility. **β2** = 2 lungs, bronchodilate.
  • Epi binds **alpha AND beta**. Albuterol = β2. Beta blockers = block β1. Dose: **not in his notes / don’t invent.**
  • Baroreceptors **exactly two:** **aorta** + **carotids**. Chemo: O2, CO2, sugar, pH.
  • Fight-or-flight **non-specific** (bleed / weak pump / anaphylaxis dilate).
  • Sympathetic = fight/flight (sacrifice GI + sex blood). Para = rest/digest / feed-and-breed.
  • CNS = brain + cord. PNS = rest. Motor out / sensory in. Somatic = voluntary. Autonomic = involuntary.
  • Brainstem (don’t split): **BP + breathing**. Cerebrum = us / seizures / strokes. Cerebellum = coordination/balance (EMT doesn’t chase disorders).
  • Broca’s **left frontal** = speech production. Occipital = vision. Temporal = understand words. Recognize **stroke**, not lobe. **No Tx until CT.** Scale/windows: **not in his notes / don’t invent.**
  • Hypothalamus: **temp**. RAS: **consciousness** gatekeeper.
  • CSF cushions / dissipates energy (also amniotic / water barrels).
  • JVD = backup / too much pressure on heart (R-side inflow fail). Flat jugulars = empty tank. **Two book conditions unnamed — don’t invent.**
  • Cord via **foramen magnum**. Motor down / sensory up. Paralysis **below**. **Phrenic C3–5** = diaphragm.
  • Homeostasis he said: HR **60–100**, RR **12–20**, BGL **80–120**, SpO2 **94–99**.

    • **[Patient Treatment and Transport]**

  • Burns: 1st **superficial** epidermis; 2nd **partial** into dermis; 3rd **full** all layers. Rule of nines: **not in his notes / don’t invent.**

    • **[Secondary Assessment]**

  • SI = nutrient absorption (duodenum/jejunum/ileum — don’t keep splitting). Peristalsis = smooth-muscle waves; leak can paralyze.
  • Appendix = **hollow** tail of large intestine; appendicitis → **lower quadrant** (later RLQ in 11:27).
  • Pain: onset, better/worse, describe, where, radiation. Patient’s L/R.
  • Pancreas: mid-upper, radiates **back**. Insulin **↓** BGL; glucagon **↑**. Diabetes Tx: **not in his notes / don’t invent.**
  • Liver RUQ solid: detox, bile (fat), clotting factors — **cut**. Rest: **not in his notes / don’t invent.**

    Saturday Ch 8 moving — NREMT key (drill)


    **FLAG: 2:01 Otter cap.** Last ~14 min **not in his notes / don’t invent.**


    **Sources:** 1:01 + 1:32 + 2:01 lecture/guides only.


    **[Operations]**

  • Study **when to use / not use** each device. Not a huge national-test chapter.
  • Gurney: all transports; **not stairs / not off-road**; head-first; head heavier; lock to floor; everyone on the gurney.
  • Powered 40–145 lb. Two hands (front + back). Curb **straight**.
  • Backboard = full-body splint. X torso + hips + legs. 6–7 ft plastic.
  • Board if: neck/back pain; numbness/tingling/weakness/paralysis; **unconscious** trauma; **poor historian**.
  • Too wide: might worsen — justify or **med control**.
  • Log roll: **four people**, head in charge, **collar first**, don’t lift by the head.
  • Lift with legs; shoulders over pelvis; **power grip palms up ~10 in**.
  • Lift assist = extra **people**, not a second ambulance. His shop ~**250 lb** two-person.
  • Bariatric problem is **width** (regular ~750 lb; he said ~1200). Keep **low**. Lift-gate **or** ramp + **winch**.
  • **Diamond carry:** 4 sides preferred.
  • Stair chair: **conscious**, no spine, no elevator. Unconscious stairs: **board, feet first**.
  • Emergency = **seconds**, skip spine (fire / pulseless in wreck / CPR).
  • Urgent ≈ **one minute**, still incomplete immobilization (~2+ min even when fast).
  • Non-urgent = stable; least lift.
  • Vest/KED-type: sitting in car, rare, stable + conscious + C-collar.
  • Vacuum mattress: beanbag + suction; **kyphosis**; rare locally; unsure → med control.
  • Stokes: raised sides, water/rope/Camelback. Board-in-basket possible.
  • Scoop: split; **hip fx**; 50%/12 mo stat he heard.
  • Isolette: neonate **0–1 month**; flight crew’s patient; he just drove / secured the vehicle.

    • **[Patient Treatment and Transport]**

  • No medical need → **position of comfort**.
  • Shock four: **lots of O2, warm, supine, rapid transport**.
  • Shock **now flat** (no feet-up).
  • Pregnancy **>20 weeks** = **left side** (IVC/aorta sandwich = **supine hypotensive syndrome**; also faces the bench).
  • Unresponsive + breathing + no spine = **recovery** (tongue).
  • Seizure recovering + breathing = recovery; else back + suction.
  • Fowler / high / semi = sit; **dyspnea** likes up.
  • Restrain only if hurting self/others; **5 people**; **supine not prone**; rule out O2/sugar/head first; document or it reads like kidnapping; monitor ABCs.

    • **[Scene Size-up and Safety]**

  • BSI / universal precautions every time.
  • Ask **we** before a lift. Stronger/taller → **head** of a manual gurney.
  • After 28:05 / last ~14 min: **not in his notes / don’t invent.**
